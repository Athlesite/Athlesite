# Example photos: where they come from

The 12 examples without built-in photos use **stock photos from Pexels** (chosen 2026-11-10 by Claude; Willy
gave the go-ahead to "scour the internet"). Jordan Bell, Maya Rodriguez and Tyler Brooks keep their own photos.

**License:** the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use, commercial use allowed, no
attribution required, can be edited. Not allowed: selling unaltered copies, implying the people shown endorse
something, or showing them in a bad light.

**How they were chosen (so real people aren't presented as these made-up athletes):** the athlete's face is
hidden, turned away, in silhouette or far away wherever possible; where a face shows (Cole, Kenzie, Sofia, some
gallery shots) it's either a professional stock shoot or an action frame at a public event. No logos or real
team names as the subject. These are **for the design prototype and the homepage examples**; before a public
launch, CFul and Willy should decide whether to keep stock, commission photos, or use AI images
(`PHOTO-PROMPTS.md`), and check the licence on each page again.

Jordan Bell's gallery photo 2 (it showed team wordmarks and helmet logos) is now the helmet shot from the
homepage's own images (`homepage-v2/assets/img/football-helmet.webp`).
