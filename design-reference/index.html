Athlete pages

The 15 example pages, three ways

Fictional athletes, one per sport. They show where the product can go, and some fields aren’t approved for public pages yet.

  • Full: every section filled in (future features included)
  • Published today: only the product’s 18 public fields
  • Minor: first name + last initial, no city, height or weight
